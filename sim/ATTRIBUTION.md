# Attribution

The apartment environment is derived from ["Appartement"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/appartement-6a7a5fe208344b2e8123a88923dbd5b3) by [SrMonteiro](https://sketchfab.com/crispimrafael), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes were made: split per room, convex-decomposed for collision, re-exported for rendering (GLB/MuJoCo meshes), and rasterized into a navigation map.

The scenario human is derived from ["Casual Man In Navy T-shirt And Jeans"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/casual-man-in-navy-t-shirt-and-jeans-bddc55b5a9d4406b982ec6de9b99531b) by [restore50](https://sketchfab.com/restore50), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes were made for simulator use: converted to a normalized MuJoCo OBJ (Y-up centimeters, feet at origin) with the base-color texture extracted; the source GLB is also redistributed for viewer rendering.

The household residents are derived from ["Executive in a Gray Suit"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/executive-in-a-gray-suit-fcf37c2c2d764e74b6750ad60f4670f9), ["Inked Warrior"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/inked-warrior-f20e27c6d3544ab8aa599f2def865ce5), and ["Casual Confidence Girl"](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/casual-confidence-girl-9f7d1343f3ae4da1b3cf7cb1795379e1) by [restore50](https://sketchfab.com/restore50), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes were made for simulator use: meshes were simplified, normalized to metres with Z up and feet at the origin, reduced to a single 1024 px base-color texture, and optimized separately as OBJ files for MuJoCo and GLB files for the browser viewer.
