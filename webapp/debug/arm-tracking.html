Arm tracking error

Records what the arm was told to hold against where its encoders actually landed, so gravity compensation can be judged by a number instead of by eye. Record one session with it on and one with it off, doing roughly the same motions. The figure that matters is the held error: a moving arm trails its setpoint by a lag compensation does not address, and averaging that in buries the effect.

Pose sweep — moves the arm

Sag is a steady-state error, so it only shows up while the arm is holding. Hand teleop never stops moving, which measures tracking lag instead. This drives the arm through a fixed set of poses, pausing at each one, and records only that — then repeats the identical poses for the second run, so the two are comparable. It starts at the zero pose, arm straight out, and works through poses that load the shoulder, elbow and wrist over the widest range the joint limits allow, each one keeping the gripper clear of the floor. The gripper itself is left alone. Clear the space in front of the robot, and stop any other teleop source first: whoever publishes last wins.
